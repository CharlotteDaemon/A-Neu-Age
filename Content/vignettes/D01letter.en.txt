#MESSAGE FROM OFFICIAL NOTARY

#_Dear Caladia,_

_Both as your professor and as well your friend, I am very proud to see you have graduated, and quite ahead of schedule! I do want this letter to encourage you to chase your dreams, however, knowing you as well as I do, I want to warn you of overambition; One should not enter a marathon just after learning to walk._

_Regardless, I know whatever may lay in your path ahead, you will assuredly be capable of overcoming it, given enough forethought and dedication. It is not incorrect for me to say I am eager to see what comes from what I consider to be my brightest student--regardless of what the board may think of your minor delinquencies--and expect you to possibly even surpass my expertise in alchemy some day._

_Attatched to this letter will be your final degree, itemized list of debts, and official certification to operate and manufacture alchemic processes and engines._

_Best of Wishes,_

*Volusia Camillus*
*Head Professor of Metallurgical Division*
*College of Alchemical Engineering*
*Aldora State University*
